Our Cupcake Recipes

Cupcakes are like individual cakes made in muffin tins and topped with frosting.

American Buttercream Frosting is the easiest style of buttercream to make with very few ingredients and steps. It is the buttercream most commonly used by home bakers.

American buttercream is typically a ratio of two parts sugar to one part butter creamed together with a small amount of milk and flavorings. It is slightly ivory in color, thick in texture and pipes easily.

To make American Buttercream:

It is important to properly cream the butter and sugar to form the buttercream. The butter must start at room temperature. If the butter is too cold the emulsion might break, causing a separated buttercream.

If the buttercream is not used right away, it must be stored in the refrigerator. It will firm up and should be allowed to come to room temperature again before spreading or piping. You may want to remix your buttercream depending on how long you stored it.

You can add color and flavors to your buttercream.

Vanilla American Buttercream: Add in 2 tsp vanilla extract.

Chocolate American Buttercream: Add 1 cup of sifted unsweetened cocoa powder.

Lemon American Buttercream: Add 3 TBSP lemon zest and ¼ cup lemon juice. Remove the milk and increase the confectioner’s sugar to 9 cups.

Strawberry, Raspberry, or Blackberry American Buttercream: Fold in 1 cup of seedless preserves.
