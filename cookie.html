Our Cookie Recipes

There are so many kinds of delicious, delectable cookies out there just waiting to be made in your kitchen. Depending on what kind of cookie you are making, here are a few basics to get you started.

Sifting dry ingredients like flour, baking powder, baking soda and cocoa powder combine and aerate them, aiding in a fluffier cookie.

Unlike stirring or mixing, creaming isn’t about combining ingredients. It’s about aerating them. Creaming creates air pockets that expand in the oven, making the dough tender. When creaming the butter and sugar, remember the butter should be at room temperature.

Eggs should be added at room temperature. To quickly warm eggs, place them in a bowl of warm water for a few minutes.
